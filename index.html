Common Questions.

What Areas Do You Serve?

Mahoney Plumbing & Heating serves Worcester, Middlesex, Norfolk, and Suffolk Counties, including Boston. See all towns and neighborhoods.

Do You Take Emergency Plumbing Calls?

Yes. Emergency plumbing is available 24/7. Call (508) 579-4968.

What Services Can I Request?

Residential plumbing, drain cleaning, water heaters, boilers, ductless mini-splits, PEX, copper, and cast iron piping, and commercial plumbing. Browse services.

Do You Work On New Construction And Remodels?

Yes. Mahoney Plumbing & Heating provides plumbing services for new construction, remodels, and renovations.

Can You Help Commercial Properties?

Yes. Visit the commercial plumbing page or call to discuss the property and work needed.

Are Your Plumber Credentials Verified?

Jonathan Mahoney holds a Massachusetts Journeyman Plumber license. See the license details and book service.

Which Payment Methods Do You Accept?

Cash App, Apple Pay, Venmo, cash, and check.

Do You Offer Free Estimates?

Yes. Estimates are free for all services, and on-site estimate visits are included.

What Is Included In An Estimate?

Estimates include labor, parts, equipment, permits, and disposal.

How Long Is An Estimate Valid?

Estimates are valid for one year.

What Warranty Do You Offer On Labor?

Labor is covered by a two-year warranty.

No. Financing and payment plans are not currently offered.
