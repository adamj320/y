Common boiler problems

Having Trouble Keeping Your Property Warm?

If your boiler is not working as expected, contact Mahoney Plumbing & Heating to discuss the symptoms.

  • Boiler not producing heat
  • Radiators or heating zones not warming
  • Uneven temperatures throughout the property
  • Boiler pressure repeatedly dropping
  • Water leaking around the boiler or connected piping, including cast iron pipework
  • System repeatedly shutting down
