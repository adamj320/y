When to call for drain service

Is A Slow Drain Becoming A Bigger Problem?

If a drain keeps slowing down, repeatedly clogs, or backs up, contact us to discuss the issue.

  • A sink, tub or shower drains slowly
  • A drain clogs again after being cleared
  • More than one fixture drains slowly
  • Water backs up into a sink, tub or floor drain
  • Gurgling sounds come from pipes or drains
  • Unpleasant odors come from a drain

Common questions

Drain Cleaning FAQs

What should I tell you about a slow or clogged drain?

Let us know which fixture is affected, how long the drain has been slow or blocked, and whether more than one fixture is involved.

Can you help when several drains are affected?

Yes. Mention that multiple fixtures are slow or backing up when you call or send a request. That helps explain the issue before the service conversation.

Do you handle drain pipe repair as well as cleaning?

Mahoney Plumbing & Heating can discuss drain cleaning, drain and pipe repair, including cast iron drain lines, and new drain piping in PVC or cast iron.
