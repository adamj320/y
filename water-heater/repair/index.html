When to call for repair

Not Getting The Hot Water You Need?

Changes in hot water or signs of a leak can point to a water heater problem. Share what has changed so Mahoney Plumbing & Heating can discuss the next step.

  • No hot water or not enough hot water
  • Water temperature changes unexpectedly
  • Water leaking near the tank or connections
  • Popping, rumbling, or other unusual sounds
  • Hot water runs out faster than usual
  • A tank or tankless unit stops working reliably

Common questions

Repair FAQs

Do you repair tankless water heaters?

Yes. Mahoney Plumbing & Heating can discuss repair for tankless as well as gas and electric tank water heaters.

What should I do if my water heater is leaking?

Keep clear of water near electrical equipment. Call Mahoney Plumbing & Heating to describe where the leak is and discuss what to do next.

What information helps with a repair request?

Share the water heater type and fuel, what symptoms you have noticed, and your town. If you know the equipment age or model, that can also help.
