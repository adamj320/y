When to call for service

Is Your Tankless System Acting Differently?

Changes in hot water or system operation can be a reason to discuss service. Tell us what you notice and when it happens.

  • No hot water or not enough hot water
  • Water temperature changes unexpectedly
  • Hot water flow is inconsistent
  • An error code or warning appears
  • Water is leaking around the unit or connections
  • The system shuts off or struggles to keep up
